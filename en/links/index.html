Mission design & analysis tools

  • NASA Space Mission Design Tools↗

    NASA's directory of tools for trajectory design, optimization, constellation analysis, and mission operations.

  • GMAT↗

    NASA's open-source tool for mission design and trajectory optimization, from Earth orbit to deep space.

  • Ansys STK↗

    Commercial software for analyzing orbits, trajectories, coverage, and communications in mission scenarios.

  • NAIF / SPICE↗

    NASA/JPL data and tools for computing spacecraft and solar system geometry, orientation, and observation conditions.

  • JPL Horizons↗

    An ephemeris service for solar system positions, velocities, and observing conditions.

Journals & technical reports

  • Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics↗

    The AIAA journal covering guidance, navigation, control, and flight dynamics.

  • Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets↗

    The AIAA journal covering spacecraft and rocket design, systems, and missions.

  • Acta Astronautica↗

    The International Academy of Astronautics journal covering space exploration and space technology.

  • Astrodynamics↗

    Research on orbital dynamics, trajectory optimization, space mission design, satellite constellations, and formation flying.

  • Advances in Space Research↗

    The COSPAR journal covering orbital dynamics, space debris, planetary exploration, and broader space science and technology.

  • Aerospace Science and Technology↗

    Research on flight mechanics, guidance, navigation, control, and spacecraft and satellite systems.

  • Chinese Journal of Aeronautics↗

    Aerospace engineering research spanning flight mechanics, vehicle design, control, and materials.

  • IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems↗

    The IEEE journal covering satellite constellations, navigation, and the design, integration, and operation of space systems.

  • IEEE Transactions on Communications↗

    The IEEE journal covering communication theory, system design, and performance analysis, including satellite and optical communications.

  • NASA Technical Reports Server↗

    Search NASA technical reports, research publications, and conference papers.
