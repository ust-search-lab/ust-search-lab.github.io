Research

Grounded in orbital mechanics and astrodynamics, SEARCH Lab studies space mission design, trajectory design and optimization, optimal control, planetary entry, descent, and landing technology, solar sail-based deep-space missions, and CubeSat-class satellite development and in-orbit demonstration missions.

Space Exploration Architecture and Mission Design

We design the overall structure of lunar, Martian, and deep-space exploration missions and connect mission objectives with system requirements. Mission concepts, trajectories, operations scenarios, communication conditions, landing conditions, and in-orbit demonstration strategies are examined in an integrated way.

Key topics

  • Conceptual design of space exploration missions
  • Derivation of mission and system requirements
  • Mission scenarios and concepts of operations
  • Integrated analysis of spacecraft orbit, communication, and landing conditions
  • Mission architecture studies linked to national space development programs

Astrodynamics and Deep-Space Trajectory Design

Building on orbital mechanics and astrodynamics, we design and analyze trajectories for lunar, Martian, and deep-space spacecraft. Taking into account limited propellant, time, launch conditions, and communication conditions, we derive trajectories and maneuver strategies that make a mission feasible.

Key topics

  • Orbital mechanics and astrodynamics
  • Lunar and planetary transfer and orbit insertion strategies
  • Deep-space trajectory design
  • Orbit propagation and perturbation analysis
  • Mission feasibility and performance analysis

Mission Optimization and Optimal Control

We apply optimization and optimal control techniques to improve space mission performance. Considering flight time, propellant consumption, landing accuracy, communication conditions, and mission risk, we develop more efficient and reliable mission design methods.

Key topics

  • Optimal control-based trajectory design
  • Trajectory optimization and sensitivity analysis
  • Low-thrust and continuous-thrust orbit transfer
  • Optimization of landing guidance and deceleration scenarios
  • Mission design methodologies based on numerical optimization

Lunar and Planetary Exploration Mission Design

We conduct mission design studies for future exploration missions, including lunar landers, Mars orbiters, Mars landers, and deep-space probes. Exploration objectives, orbits, landing sites, observation geometry, communication geometry, and operational constraints are considered together.

Key topics

  • Lunar exploration and lunar landing mission design
  • Mars orbiter and lander mission design
  • Interplanetary transfer and orbit insertion strategies
  • Landing site, observation geometry, and communication geometry analysis
  • Operations scenario design for exploration missions

Planetary Entry, Descent, and Landing Technology

We study entry, descent, and landing (EDL) technologies that allow a spacecraft entering a planetary atmosphere, such as that of Mars, to decelerate and land safely. Atmospheric models, aerothermodynamics, parachute deployment conditions, guidance and control, and landing dispersion are analyzed from a mission design perspective.

Key topics

  • Mars atmospheric entry trajectory analysis
  • Entry guidance and parachute deployment condition analysis
  • Deceleration and landing scenario design
  • 3-DOF/6-DOF EDL simulation
  • Aerothermodynamics, atmospheric model, and landing dispersion analysis
  • Performance verification of planetary landing missions

CubeSat-Class Satellite Development and In-Orbit Demonstration

To carry the mission concepts and analysis results developed in the lab through to in-orbit demonstration, we study CubeSat-class satellite development and operations concepts. Student researchers gain experience not only in mission design but also in system requirements, ground testing, operations scenarios, and space environment verification.

Key topics

  • CubeSat-class satellite mission design
  • In-orbit demonstration mission planning
  • Satellite concepts of operations
  • Derivation of mission and system requirements
  • Ground testing and space environment verification
  • In-orbit demonstration linked to national R&D programs

Solar Sail and Future Deep-Space Mission Design

We study solar sail astrodynamics driven by solar radiation pressure, long-duration low-thrust flight, and future deep-space mission design. Solar sailing is a future spaceflight technology that enables long-duration deep-space missions while minimizing propellant use.

Key topics

  • Solar sail-based orbit raising and deep-space mission design
  • Astrodynamics under solar radiation pressure
  • Long-duration low-thrust trajectory design
  • Non-Keplerian and special orbit design
  • Conceptual design of future exploration missions
  • Solar sail in-orbit demonstration mission planning
