A research team led by Dr. Jae-ik Park of the Korea Aerospace Research Institute (KARI) attended the 2026 Fall Conference of the Korean Space Science Society (KSSS), held at the Sheraton Jeju Hotel in Jeju, Korea, from October 27 to 30, 2026.

On October 28, the second day of the conference, a special session was held for the Mars atmospheric entry, descent, and landing (EDL) core technology development project, which is carried out under the Space Challenge program of the Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA).

The session, organized by principal investigator Dr. Park, was divided into two parts on Mars exploration EDL technology, held in the morning and afternoon. Researchers from the project's participating institutions presented a total of 14 papers: KARI as the lead institution; the Korea Institute of Ceramic Engineering and Technology (KICET), DACC Carbon, and Yonsei University as joint research institutions; and Seoul National University and KAIST as commissioned research institutions.
