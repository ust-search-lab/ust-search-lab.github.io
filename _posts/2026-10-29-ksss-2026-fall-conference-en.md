Dr. Jae-ik Park visited the Sheraton Jeju Hotel in Jeju, Korea, from October 27 to 29, 2026, to attend the 2026 Fall Conference of the Korean Space Science Society (KSSS).

On October 28, a special session for the Mars atmospheric entry, descent, and landing (EDL) core technology development project was held in Ballroom III. The project is carried out under the Space Challenge program of the Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA). Researchers from the four project institutions presented 14 papers: the Korea Aerospace Research Institute (KARI) as the lead institution, and the Korea Institute of Ceramic Engineering and Technology (KICET), DACC Carbon, and Yonsei University as joint research institutions.

Dr. Park, the project's principal investigator, organized the special session, which ran as two sessions on Mars exploration EDL technology in the morning and afternoon. Dr. Park and the lab's collaborators all attended and presented papers.
