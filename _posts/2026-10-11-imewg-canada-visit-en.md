SEARCH Lab's **Dr. Jae-ik Park**, together with collaborators **Dr. Jae-In Kim and Dr. Jungju Bae**, will travel to **Montréal, Canada, from October 12 to 18, 2026**, to attend the **International Mars Exploration Working Group (IMEWG) in-person meeting**.

This meeting is held once a year and will take place at the **Canadian Space Agency (CSA)** this year.
