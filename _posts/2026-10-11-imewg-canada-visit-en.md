SEARCH Lab's **Dr. Jae-ik Park** is attending the **International Mars Exploration Working Group (IMEWG) in-person meeting** together with collaborators **Dr. Jae-In Kim and Dr. Jungju Bae** during their **October 12–18, 2026 visit to Montréal, Canada**.

Held annually, this year's in-person meeting is taking place at the **Canadian Space Agency (CSA)**.
