SEARCH Lab's **Dr. Jae-ik Park**, together with collaborators **Dr. Jae-In Kim and Dr. Jungju Bae**, completed a visit to **Montréal, Canada, from October 12 to 18, 2026**.

They attended the **annual International Mars Exploration Working Group (IMEWG) in-person meeting as part of the Korean delegation alongside representatives of the Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA)**. This year's meeting was held at the **Canadian Space Agency (CSA)**.
