Dr. Jae-ik Park was appointed as a judge for the 2026 Korea Space Rover Challenge, held on October 23–24 at the Sacheon Airshow venue in Gyeongsangnam-do, Korea, and attended the event.

Despite rain and chilly weather, the participating teams stood out for their innovative designs and enthusiasm. KAIST's Team STAR took first place and received the Grand Prize, the KASA Administrator's Award.

It was a meaningful occasion that showed the outstanding technical skills and bold spirit of the students who will lead future space exploration.
