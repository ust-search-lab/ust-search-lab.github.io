SEARCH Lab's **Dr. Jae-ik Park** served as a **judge** at the **2026 Korea Space Rover Challenge**, held on October 23–24 at the **Sacheon Airshow venue at Sacheon Airport** in Gyeongsangnam-do, Korea.

At this year's competition, held alongside the Sacheon Airshow, **KAIST's Team STAR** took **first place**, receiving the **Grand Prize (KASA Administrator's Award)**.

The event offered an opportunity to see a variety of ideas for space exploration rovers and the students' enthusiasm. Their efforts to turn ideas into real rovers and explain their approaches were especially memorable.

Rain throughout the day and cold conditions made the event challenging, but the students continued their efforts. We thank all participating teams and the organizers for their enthusiasm and commitment in difficult weather.
