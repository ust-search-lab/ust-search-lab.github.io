SEARCH Lab's Dr. Jae-ik Park visited Sacheon Airport as a judge for the 2026 Korea Space Rover Challenge, held on October 23–24 at the Sacheon Airshow venue.

It rained all day and was quite cold. The conditions were difficult, but looking at the students' rovers was a pleasure. Each had different ideas behind it, and it was clear how much work the students had put into preparing for the competition.

KAIST's Team STAR took first place and received the Grand Prize, the KASA Administrator's Award. Congratulations, Team STAR!

A big thank-you to all the students and organizers who spent the day out in the rain and cold.
