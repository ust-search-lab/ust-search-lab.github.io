Dr. Jae-ik Park was appointed Associate Professor in Aerospace System Engineering at the University of Science and Technology (UST), effective September 1, 2026. The appointment marked the launch of SEARCH Lab (Space Exploration ARCHitecture Laboratory) and its website.

SEARCH Lab draws on orbital mechanics and astrodynamics to study missions from Earth orbit to the Moon, planets, and deep space. The lab develops mission concepts, orbits, and trajectories, assesses mission feasibility through optimization and systems analysis, and aims to connect research with in-space demonstration.

The website presents [information about the lab]({{ '/en/about/' | relative_url }}), its [research areas]({{ '/en/research/' | relative_url }}), [research outputs]({{ '/en/publications/' | relative_url }}), and information for prospective students. It also carries updates on lab activities and news.

## Graduate study and research inquiries

The [For Students]({{ '/en/students/' | relative_url }}) page outlines foundational study, the research process, and degree-level goals for prospective master's and doctoral students in space mission design and technology demonstration.

Graduate study and research participation inquiries can be submitted through [Contact]({{ '/en/contact/' | relative_url }}), together with a brief description of research interests and plans for graduate study.
