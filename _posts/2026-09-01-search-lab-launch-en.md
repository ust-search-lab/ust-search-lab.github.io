Following Dr. Jae-ik Park's appointment as Associate Professor in Aerospace System Engineering at the University of Science and Technology (UST), effective September 1, 2026, **SEARCH Lab (Space Exploration ARCHitecture Laboratory)** officially launched and opened its website.

Building on orbital mechanics and astrodynamics, SEARCH Lab designs and studies a wide range of space missions, from low Earth orbit to the Moon, planets, and deep space. Through mission concept development, trajectory optimization, and systems analysis, the lab assesses the feasibility of space missions and aims to carry its research results through to in-space demonstration.

The official website will continue to share the lab's [vision]({{ '/en/about/' | relative_url }}), main [research areas]({{ '/en/research/' | relative_url }}), [research outputs]({{ '/en/publications/' | relative_url }}), and latest activities.

## Graduate study and research participation

SEARCH Lab recruits master's and doctoral student researchers with a passion for space mission design and in-space demonstration research on a rolling basis. Research roadmaps for each degree program and further details are available on the [For Students]({{ '/en/students/' | relative_url }}) page. Those who would like to discuss graduate study or research participation can get in touch by [email]({{ '/en/contact/' | relative_url }}), including their research interests and plans for graduate study.
