UST **Spring 2027 first-round applications** run from **September 16, 2026 at 10:00 to October 6 at 15:00 (KST)**.

## Application information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Admission tracks | General admission and the Professional Master's program |
| Applications open | September 16, 2026 (Wednesday), 10:00 |
| Application deadline | **October 6, 2026 (Tuesday), 15:00** |
| How to apply | Online through the [UST Admissions System](https://apply.ust.ac.kr/) |

All times are Korea Standard Time (KST). This summary follows the [official Korean-language announcement and attached admissions guidelines](https://www.ust.ac.kr/bbs/BBSMSTR_000000000757/view.do?nttId=B000000012830Yz5kH2&mno=sub06_01_01). Check the guidelines for eligibility, available majors and degrees, required documents, and the full selection schedule.

## Graduate study and research inquiries

To discuss research topics and master's or doctoral study at SEARCH Lab, please [contact us]({{ '/en/contact/' | relative_url }}) with your research interests, academic background, and intended degree.

For application procedures, contact the UST Admissions Office at [k_adm@ust.ac.kr](mailto:k_adm@ust.ac.kr) or +82-42-865-2424.
