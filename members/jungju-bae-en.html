  • Space Exploration Team

Biography

Dr. Bae studies spacecraft dynamics and space exploration mission and system design, grounded in dynamics and control in mechanical engineering.

Doctoral research focused on the orbital and attitude dynamics, stability, and control of solar sail spacecraft, as well as interplanetary flight. At the Korea Aerospace Research Institute (KARI), Dr. Bae now conducts research on planetary exploration and space systems, expanding into Mars entry, descent, and landing (EDL), flight environment analysis in very low Earth orbit, and conceptual design of space exploration systems.
